🛕 Sacred Temples of Uttarakhand

Discover the divine history of Devभूमि

Kedarnath Temple

One of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, Kedarnath Temple is located in the Garhwal Himalayas. It is believed to have been built by the Pandavas and later revived by Adi Shankaracharya. Surrounded by snow-covered peaks, it symbolizes devotion, endurance, and spiritual strength.

Badrinath Temple

Dedicated to Lord Vishnu, Badrinath is part of the Char Dham pilgrimage. Established by Adi Shankaracharya, it lies along the Alaknanda River. The temple represents purity, salvation, and divine knowledge.

Gangotri Temple

Gangotri Temple marks the origin of the sacred River Ganga. According to legend, Goddess Ganga descended here from heaven. It is a major pilgrimage site symbolizing purity and life.

Yamunotri Temple

Dedicated to Goddess Yamuna, this temple is the starting point of Char Dham. It is known for its hot springs and spiritual significance. Devotees believe it grants protection from untimely death.

Jageshwar Dham

A cluster of over 100 ancient temples dedicated to Lord Shiva. Dating back to the 9th century, it showcases rich stone architecture. It is believed to be one of the earliest Jyotirlinga sites.

Tungnath Temple

The highest Shiva temple in the world, located at 3,680 meters. It is part of the Panch Kedar temples built by the Pandavas. Known for breathtaking views and spiritual calmness.

Neelkanth Mahadev

Located near Rishikesh, this temple marks the place where Lord Shiva consumed poison. It is surrounded by dense forests and holds great mythological importance.

Baijnath Temple

Built in the 12th century, Baijnath Temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva as Vaidyanath. Situated near the Gomti River, it reflects ancient Kumaoni architecture.
